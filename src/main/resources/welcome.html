How it works

A quick tour of what you can do here.

1

Discover Art

Browse the Gallery to see artwork from the community. Use the search bar or category filters to find something specific.

2

Publish Your Work

Head to Create to upload your own artwork, add a title, description, and category. You'll need to log in first.

3

Save Your Favorites

Found something you love? Tap the 🔖 Save button on any artwork in the Gallery, and find it later on your Saved page.

4

Make It Yours

Open Settings and pick a theme — Pink, Light, Dark, or your own custom color — to make the site feel like home.
